# Advanced Quest 1 – AI Adoption Playbook

## Project Overview
This project presents a 90-day AI adoption playbook for a fictional small business, **Brew & Bloom Café**, with 25 employees in the food and beverage industry.

The objective is to introduce AI into everyday business operations through a structured, responsible, and measurable implementation plan.

## Business Details
- Business: Brew & Bloom Café
- Industry: Food and Beverage
- Team Size: 25 Employees
- Project Duration: 90 Days

## Key Areas Covered

1. **Approved AI Tools** – ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Canva Magic Studio, Microsoft Excel with AI features, and Google Gemini.
2. **AI Business Applications** – Marketing, customer support, sales, inventory, HR, and management.
3. **Human Review Rules** – Accuracy checks, approval procedures, and human accountability.
4. **Brand Voice Guidelines** – Friendly, warm, simple, and consistent communication.
5. **Data Protection** – Customer privacy, confidential information handling, and responsible AI usage.
6. **Employee Training** – Prompt writing, output verification, and safe AI practices.
7. **90-Day Roadmap** – Preparation and pilot, department implementation, and evaluation.
8. **Success Metrics** – Time saved, employee adoption, output quality, and data compliance.

## 90-Day Implementation Plan

| Phase | Timeline | Main Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Days 1–30 | Preparation and pilot |
| Phase 2 | Days 31–60 | Department implementation |
| Phase 3 | Days 61–90 | Evaluation and improvement |

## Deliverable
The project document contains the complete AI Adoption Playbook, including the proposed tools, business use cases, review rules, data policies, and implementation roadmap.

**Note:** Brew & Bloom Café is a fictional business used for this case study. The playbook is a proposed adoption plan, not a report of an already implemented rollout.
