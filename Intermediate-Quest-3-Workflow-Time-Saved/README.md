# Intermediate Quest 3: Map a Workflow and Measure Time Saved

## Project Overview

This project maps a small-business workflow and evaluates how Artificial Intelligence can improve efficiency by reducing the time required to complete tasks.

The selected workflow is preparing and publishing a weekly promotional post on Instagram for a fictional small business called Brew & Bloom Café.

## Business Scenario

- Business: Brew & Bloom Café
- Workflow: Weekly Instagram promotional post
- Product: Cold coffee
- Price: ₹120
- Target audience: Students and local customers
- Brand voice: Friendly, warm, and simple

## Workflow Steps

1. Decide the weekly offer.
2. Collect product and price details.
3. Prepare the Instagram caption.
4. Select relevant hashtags.
5. Create a promotional image.
6. Review the content.
7. Publish the Instagram post.

## AI Integration

AI was used to support:
- Instagram caption generation.
- Hashtag suggestions.
- Promotional wording.

Human review was retained for checking factual accuracy, brand voice, and final content approval.

## Before-and-After Time Comparison

| Workflow | Before AI | After AI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Decide weekly offer | 10 min | 10 min |
| Collect/provide details | 8 min | 3 min |
| Prepare caption | 12 min | 4 min |
| Select hashtags | 10 min | 4 min |
| Create image | 5 min | 3 min |
| Review content | 5 min | 5 min |
| Publish post | 5 min | 5 min |
| **Total** | **55 min** | **34 min** |

## Time-Saving Results

- Before AI: 55 minutes
- After AI: 34 minutes
- Time saved: 21 minutes
- Time reduction: 38.2%

Formula:

Time Saved = Before AI − After AI

Time Reduction = (Time Saved ÷ Before AI) × 100

## Key Learning

AI can reduce the time required for content preparation by assisting with captions and hashtags. Human review remains important to ensure accuracy, appropriate brand communication, and final approval.

## Conclusion

This workflow demonstrates how AI can be integrated into a small-business process. The illustrative comparison shows a reduction from 55 minutes to 34 minutes.

## Methodology Note

The timings shown are illustrative estimates prepared for the learning exercise. They should be replaced with actual stopwatch measurements after testing the manual and AI-assisted workflows.
