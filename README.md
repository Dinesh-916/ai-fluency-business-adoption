# AI Fluency & Building – AI Adoption

## Task 1: Where AI Helps a Business

### Project Overview
This project explores how Artificial Intelligence can help small businesses improve productivity, automate repetitive activities, and make better operational decisions.

### Business Type
Cafe / Restaurant

### Areas of AI Adoption

**1. Customer Inquiries**
- AI-powered chatbots
- Automated responses to frequently asked questions
- Customer support assistance

**2. Social Media Marketing**
- AI-generated marketing captions
- Promotional content ideas
- Social media content planning

**3. Inventory Management**
- Sales data analysis
- Stock requirement forecasting
- Low-stock alerts

### Key Learning
AI can improve business efficiency, but human involvement remains important for customer relationships, quality control, and final business decisions.
