## Task 2: Good AI Habits Cheat Sheet

### Overview
This task focuses on understanding how to use Artificial Intelligence effectively, safely, and responsibly in everyday business activities.

### Key Topics Covered
1. **Give AI Context:** Provide clear background information and explain the objective.
2. **Write Clear Prompts:** Use specific instructions to get relevant results.
3. **Set Boundaries:** Define the required format, tone, length, and limitations.
4. **Verify AI Answers:** Check facts, dates, numbers, and important information.
5. **Protect Sensitive Data:** Never share passwords, OTPs, financial details, or confidential customer information.
6. **Maintain Brand Voice:** Review AI-generated content to ensure it matches the organization's communication style.
7. **Keep Humans Responsible:** Review AI outputs and make important decisions using human judgement.

### Do's and Don'ts
- **Do:** Provide context and clear instructions.
- **Do:** Verify AI-generated information before using it.
- **Do:** Protect personal and confidential data.
- **Don't:** Trust every AI response without checking.
- **Don't:** Share sensitive information with AI tools.
- **Don't:** Publish AI-generated content without human review.

### Example Prompt
"Act as a social media content assistant. Write a short, professional Instagram caption for a coffee shop promoting a new coffee product. Use a friendly tone and include a call to action."

### Key Learning
Responsible AI usage requires clear communication, fact-checking, privacy awareness, and human supervision.
