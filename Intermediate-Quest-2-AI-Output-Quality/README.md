# Intermediate Quest 2: AI Output Quality Checklist

## Project Overview

This project evaluates 20 sample AI-generated business outputs to identify quality issues in five areas:

- Accuracy
- Brand Voice
- Clarity
- Originality
- Call to Action (CTA)

## Business Scenario

The evaluation uses a fictional small business called Brew & Bloom Café.

Business reference details:
- Brand voice: Friendly, warm, and simple
- Cold coffee price: ₹120
- Opening hours: Daily, 9:00 AM–8:00 PM
- Services: Dine-in and takeaway
- Delivery: Not available

## Content Evaluated

The evaluation includes:
- 10 Instagram captions
- 8 customer emails
- 2 product descriptions

## Evaluation Method

Each output was checked against all five quality criteria.

Scoring:
- 5 = Pass
- 2 = Issue

The average score was calculated across the five criteria for each output.

## Results Summary

| Quality Check | Issues Detected |
|---|---:|
| Accuracy | 4 |
| Brand Voice | 4 |
| Clarity | 4 |
| Originality | 4 |
| CTA | 4 |
| Total criterion flags | 20 |

Average score across all 20 outputs: **4.4 / 5.0**

## Key Learning

The exercise demonstrates how AI-generated content can contain incorrect information, unsuitable tone, unclear wording, generic phrases, or missing calls to action.

Human review is important before publishing or sharing AI-generated content.

## Project Deliverables

1. AI Output Quality Checklist document.
2. Excel evaluation spreadsheet containing 20 outputs, scores, issue categories, and explanations.

## Methodology Note

This is an illustrative test set. The examples were deliberately distributed across the five issue categories. The results describe this sample only and do not represent the general error rate of AI systems.
