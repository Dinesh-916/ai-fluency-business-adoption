# Intermediate Quest 1: AI at Work Workshop

## Project Overview
This project covers a 30-minute AI awareness workshop designed for a small business team of 3–5 participants.

The workshop demonstrates practical applications of Artificial Intelligence in everyday business operations.

## Workshop Topics

### 1. Social Media Content Creation
Demonstrated how AI can generate Instagram promotional posts for a local café launching a new cold coffee.

### 2. Customer Support
Demonstrated how AI can prepare polite and professional responses to customer inquiries about delayed orders.

### 3. Weekly Business Planning
Demonstrated how AI can organize weekly activities, including inventory checking, staff scheduling, social media posting, customer feedback, cleaning, and sales tracking.

## Hands-on Activity
Participants use a prompt template to generate AI responses for tasks related to their own business activities.

## Workshop Learning Outcomes
- Understanding practical uses of AI in small businesses.
- Learning how to write effective AI prompts.
- Using AI for content creation and customer communication.
- Organizing business activities with AI.
- Understanding the importance of reviewing AI-generated results.
