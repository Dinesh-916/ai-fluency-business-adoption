# Advanced Quest 2 – Run a Real AI Pilot

## Project Overview

This project focuses on conducting a 14-day AI adoption pilot with a real small business team consisting of 3–5 participants.

The objective is to evaluate how Artificial Intelligence can support everyday business activities, improve productivity, reduce repetitive tasks, and improve work quality.

## Project Details

- Track: AI Fluency & Building – AI Adoption
- Level: Advanced – Pro
- Quest: 2 – Run a Real AI Pilot
- Duration: 14 Days
- Pilot Group: Small Business Team
- Participants: 3–5 People
- Primary AI Tool: ChatGPT

## Pilot Activities

The pilot covers five business activities:

1. Social media content creation
2. Customer communication
3. Professional email writing
4. Weekly business planning
5. Business reporting

## 14-Day Pilot Structure

| Period | Activities |
|---|---|
| Days 1–2 | Team introduction and baseline measurement |
| Days 3–6 | Initial AI task testing |
| Day 7 | First-week review |
| Days 8–10 | Improved prompt testing and business reporting |
| Days 11–12 | Quality evaluation and failure analysis |
| Days 13–14 | Final measurement and case study preparation |

## Measurement Criteria

The pilot evaluates:

- Time taken before AI
- Time taken with AI
- Total time saved
- AI output quality
- Accuracy and clarity
- Errors and corrections
- Participant feedback

## Project Deliverables

- Daily AI Usage Tracker
- AI Output Quality Evaluation
- Pilot Summary Spreadsheet
- Two-Week AI Adoption Case Study

## Expected Learning Outcomes

- Understand practical AI adoption in business.
- Identify tasks suitable for AI assistance.
- Measure productivity changes.
- Evaluate AI-generated output quality.
- Identify AI limitations and failures.
- Understand responsible AI usage.

## Important Note

This repository contains the pilot plan and project documentation. Actual results, time savings, quality scores, and participant feedback will be added after completing the real 14-day pilot.

All final findings will be based on actual observations and measurements.
